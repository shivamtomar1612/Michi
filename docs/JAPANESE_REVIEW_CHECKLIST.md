# Japanese language and cultural review checklist

This checklist is a release gate for a fluent Japanese reviewer. Automated message parity does not establish translation quality or cultural correctness.

## Language quality

- [ ] Review navigation, forms, validation, errors, empty states, dialogs, notifications, and accessibility labels in Japanese.
- [ ] Check natural phrasing, honorific level, punctuation, terminology consistency, and context-sensitive ICU messages.
- [ ] Check date, time, yen, number, and count formatting on a Japanese locale device.
- [ ] Confirm proper names and official organization/source names match their verified source records.
- [ ] Confirm text does not overflow at 320 px, 200% zoom, or long-content conditions.

## Cultural and evidence integrity

- [ ] Ensure official source passages remain distinguishable from MICHI-authored summaries and AI explanations.
- [ ] Review any translated cultural, religious, etiquette, photography, safety, accessibility, dietary, or host-rule content against its original source.
- [ ] Confirm uncertainty, stale-source, conflict, and missing-evidence warnings retain their original meaning.
- [ ] Confirm external operators are not described as MICHI partners or bookable hosts without verified authorization.

## Release decision

- [ ] Approve the exact locale and routes reviewed, with reviewer name and date recorded in the release notes.
- [ ] Block any claim of complete Japanese localization until all public, traveler, host, DMO, admin, and authentication surfaces have been reviewed.

No fluent-reviewer sign-off is recorded by this engineering change.
